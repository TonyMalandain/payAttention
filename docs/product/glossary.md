# Glossary

Terms in the user's language. The architect may add the code-side name for each.

- **Must-attend meeting** — a meeting where your absence would be noticed or would cost something. It gets alerts that escalate.
- **Ignorable meeting** — a placeholder, a personal block, a meeting you declined, a meeting marked "free", or a large broadcast where nobody notices who is missing. It gets no alerts.
- **Verdict** — the product's judgment of a meeting: must-attend or ignorable, with a one-line reason.
- **Override** — the user corrects a verdict, for one occurrence or for the whole recurring series.
- **Lead time** — how long before the start the first alert appears.
- **Travel time** — how long it takes to get to an in-person meeting. It is added to the lead time.
- **Leave-by time** — the start time minus the travel time. For an in-person meeting, escalation counts down to this, not to the start.
- **Escalation** — the alert gets stronger in steps as the deadline gets closer.
- **Takeover** — the last escalation step. It covers the screen until the user acts.
- **Acknowledge** — one action that says "I am on it". It stops the escalation for that meeting. Joining an online meeting from the alert also counts as acknowledging.
- **Online meeting** — a meeting with a video-call link or a named video platform and no physical place.
- **In-person meeting** — a meeting with a physical place and no video-call link.
