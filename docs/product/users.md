# Who touches the product

One person built this for their own use. There is one real profile. A second
profile exists only because the same person also installs and tunes it.

## Calendar owner
- **Who they are:** a busy professional on a GNOME desktop with a full calendar of video calls, in-person meetings, and placeholder blocks.
- **What they want:** to never miss a meeting that matters, while hearing nothing about the ones that do not.
- **What would make them give up:** a false alarm that takes over the screen for a meeting that did not matter, or a missed meeting that it judged unimportant with no easy way to correct it.

## Installer (the same person, wearing another hat)
- **Who they are:** the calendar owner, when they install, update, or tune the extension.
- **What they want:** install once and do almost no setup. Their calendars are already connected to the desktop.
- **What would make them give up:** a long setup, a second calendar login, or an extension that breaks the desktop session.
